How Palle Praja Works in 3 Simple Steps

1. Speak in Your Voice

No typing required. Just talk in Telugu or English and snap a photo of the damaged road, water leak, or fallen pole.

2. Automatic Assignment

Instant unique ID & automated dispatch to village engineering departments with a strict 72-hour resolution SLA.

3. Verify Before Closure

Officials must upload photographic resolution proof. Citizens audit the work and can reopen if not properly fixed.

15-Step Demo Script

Follow this exact sequence to demonstrate the end-to-end connected workflow to hackathon judges:

1-2. Language & Voice Input

Switch between English & తెలుగు. Click mic or choose "Damaged Road" preset.

3-5. Register & Submit

Click "Damaged Road" sample photo. Fill location & submit complaint.

6. Unique Complaint ID

Observe generated ID (e.g., PP-2025-RD-XXXX) & SLA target.

7-8. Official Dashboard

Switch role to "Grama Sachivalayam". Locate the submitted complaint.

9-10. Assign & In Progress

Assign to Panchayat Raj Engineering Dept & transition status to "In Progress".

11. Live Citizen Tracking

Switch back to Villager Portal -> Track Grievance. Notice the live timeline update!

12-13. Resolution Proof Upload

In Official Dashboard, upload "Repaved Road" resolution photo & mark "Resolved".

14. Citizen Verification / Reopen

In Villager Portal, inspect Before/After photos. Click "Verify & Rate" OR "Reopen Grievance".

15. Higher Authority Oversight

Switch to "District Authority". Observe Overdue SLA detection & Collector Escalation action!
