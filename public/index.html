15-Step Workflow Guide

Use this guide to explore the end-to-end connected workflow:

1-2. Language & Voice Input

Switch between English & తెలుగు. Click mic or choose "Damaged Road" preset.

3-5. Register & Submit

Optionally upload an original JPEG, PNG, or WebP complaint photo, then fill the location and submit.

6. Unique Complaint ID

Observe generated ID (e.g., PP-2025-RD-XXXX) & SLA target.

7-8. Official Dashboard

Switch role to "Grama Sachivalayam". Locate the submitted complaint.

9-10. Assign & In Progress

Sign in with a Grama Sachivalayam account, assign the complaint, add field notes, and upload inspection evidence.

11. Live Citizen Tracking

Switch back to Villager Portal -> Track Grievance. Notice the live timeline update!

12-13. Resolution Proof Upload

In the Grama Sachivalayam dashboard, upload a real repair photo, then mark the complaint "Resolved".

14. Citizen Verification / Reopen

In Villager Portal, inspect Before/After photos. Click "Verify & Rate" OR "Reopen Grievance".

15. Higher Authority Oversight

Switch to "District Authority", sign in with a District Authority account, review the school-drainage referral, and return it with a 24-hour action deadline. Sign in with the Grama Sachivalayam account to find and address the urgent return.
